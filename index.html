Everything you need on court

Score, announce, display, and track — without interrupting the game.

touch_app

Tap to Score

Full-screen split layout. Tap your team's half to award a point. Blue vs orange — impossible to miss. Made a mistake? Undo it.

bluetooth_searching

Hands-Free Scoring

Pair a Bluetooth shutter remote or up to 2 Flic buttons. Score, undo, call faults, and switch serve without touching the phone. Auto change ends swaps court sides at the right moment.

campaign

Your Personal Courtside Umpire

TTS announces every point, game, set, and match. Call faults, lets, and outs from the Say panel. English, German, and Indonesian — configurable language, voice, and speed. 4 custom sound slots with file picker and in-app recorder.

group

Put Names to the Game

Assign players to each team before every match. Persistent directory with recency and frequency suggestions. Player names appear on the scoreboard, in announcements, and on displays.

history

Match Stats & History

Tap the center overlay for set-by-set scores, serving info, and match duration. Every completed match saved — date-grouped history with full set breakdowns.

watch

Wear OS Companion

Live scoreboard on your wrist with haptic feedback. Score, undo, switch serve, and reannounce from your watch. Swipe for match stats and the Say page.

cast

Cast to the Big Screen

Cast live scores to any Chromecast-enabled TV. VS preview screen before the match starts.

grid_on

LED Courtside Display

Connect our BLE LED matrix for live courtside scoring. Team colors on 64×32 RGB displays, serve indicator, score animations, and adjustable brightness. Sides swap on the display when you change ends. Player VS preview before the match. OTA firmware updates from the app.

Your Court, Your Rules

From classic tennis scoring to casual point-based games.

format_list_bulleted

Classic

Full padel/tennis scoring with deep configuration.

  • Best of 1–5 sets
  • 3, 4, 5, or 6 games per set
  • Golden point, advantage, or star point deuce
  • Tiebreak, two-game lead, or no margin
  • Short set and sudden death options
  • Match tiebreak for even best-of sets
scoreboard

Total Games

Play a fixed number of games. Most wins takes the match.

  • 3 to 9 games
  • All games are played
  • Even totals can end in a draw
  • Golden point, advantage, or star point per game
bolt

Fixed Point

Americano-style scoring. Each tap is one point — no 15/30/40, just a race to the target.

  • 16, 21, 24, or 32 points
  • Configurable serve rotation
  • Tiebreak rules for even totals
