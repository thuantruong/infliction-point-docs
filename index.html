Everything you need on court

Score your padel matches without interrupting the game.

touch_app

Tap to Score

Full-screen split layout. Tap your team's half to award a point. Blue vs orange — impossible to miss.

bluetooth_searching

Hands-Free Scoring

Pair a Flic button or any Bluetooth shutter remote. Score, undo, and switch serve without touching the phone.

campaign

Score Announcements

Text-to-speech reads scores aloud after every point. Shake or tap to reannounce. Serving team's score first.

watch

Wear OS Companion

Live scoreboard on your wrist. Score, undo, switch serve, and view match stats — all from your watch.

undo

Undo Support

Made a mistake? Revert up to 50 scoring actions. Never lose track of the real score.

screen_rotation

Adaptive Layout

Works in landscape and portrait. Fits any phone screen — mount it, prop it, or hold it courtside.

Three match formats

From classic padel sets to casual point-based games.

format_list_bulleted

Sets

Classic padel scoring with full configuration.

  • Advantage or golden point
  • Tiebreak on/off
  • 3, 4, 5, or 6 games per set
  • Best of 1, 3, or 5 sets
scoreboard

Total Games

Play a fixed number of games. Most wins takes the match.

  • 3, 5, 7, or 9 games
  • All games are played
  • Advantage on/off per game
bolt

Fixed Point

Race to a target. Each tap adds one point — no game cycle.

  • 21, 24, or 32 points
  • Configurable serve rotation
  • Tiebreak rules for draws
